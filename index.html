Core Capabilities

Legal Metrology Compliance Features

Designed specifically for Legal Metrology Officers, enforcement agencies, and consumer goods manufacturers to streamline package label verification.

01

AI OCR Label Extraction

Multi-lingual neural optical character recognition automatically reads MRP, Net Quantity, Date of Manufacture, Expiry, Batch Number, Country of Origin, and Manufacturer Address.

  • Extracts text in 12+ official scheduled languages
  • Multi-angle & curved package label deskewing
  • Automatic Principal Display Panel (PDP) bounding box mapping
02

Compliance Validation

Evaluates extracted label attributes against Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 and latest 2024 statutory amendments in real-time.

  • Minimum font size vs package area ratio verification
  • Standard SI unit validation for Net Mass / Volume
  • Dual-pricing & e-commerce compliance check
03

Violation Detection

Instantly flags illegible font sizes, missing country of origin, hidden MRP stickers, expired shelf life, and incomplete customer helpline declarations.

  • Automated legal section mapping (Section 36 fines)
  • Risk severity categorization (Low, Moderate, Critical)
  • AI actionable recommendation generation
04

Compliance Certificate Generation

Generates tamper-evident, official digital Legal Metrology Compliance Certificates complete with unique verification hash, QR code, and downloadable PDF report.

  • Instant official certificate issuance with QR badge
  • Printable enforcement audit summary for inspectors
  • Cryptographic stamp verification for public portals

Legal Metrology Statutory Mandate

Under Section 36 of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009, whoever manufactures, packs, imports or sells any pre-packaged commodity which does not conform to the declarations on the package shall be punishable with fine up to ₹25,000 for first offense, and up to ₹1,00,000 or imprisonment for subsequent offenses.
