MRP & Price Declaration Rules

Rule 6(1)(e) & Section 36 of Legal Metrology Act, 2009

Statutory Overview

Every pre-packaged commodity must prominently declare the Maximum Retail Price (MRP) inclusive of all taxes in Indian Rupees (₹). Dual pricing for the same commodity in different geographical areas without statutory authorization is strictly illegal under the Packaged Commodities Rules.

Key Compliance Standards

  • MRP format must be: 'MRP ₹ xx.xx (incl. of all taxes)' or 'Maximum Retail Price ₹ xx.xx (inclusive of all taxes)'.
  • Stickers pasted over pre-printed MRP to inflate prices are an offense under Section 36(2).
  • E-commerce platforms must display MRP on digital product listings equal to physical package MRP.

Mandatory Minimum Font Size Specifications

Net Quantity / Package Size Minimum Height of Numeral & Letters
Up to 50g / 50ml 1.0 mm
50g to 200g / 200ml 2.0 mm
200g to 1kg / 1L 4.0 mm
Above 1kg / 1L 6.0 mm
Statutory Offenses & Penalties Schedule (Sec 36)
  • First Offense: Fine up to ₹25,000 for non-declaration or illegal sticker alteration.
  • Second Offense: Fine up to ₹50,000.
  • Subsequent Offenses: Fine up to ₹1,000,000 or imprisonment up to 1 year, or both.
